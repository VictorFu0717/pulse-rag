FAQ-Update CTID
Update CTID
The Steps of Updating CTID
1.Log in your account
2. Click your account on the top right and choose
                        "Domain Management"
3. Click “Contact Management” ＞ “Modify”
*You can press “Search” button to search domain name
                        quickly
4.Modify the contact information and click “save” after
                        confirming it is correct
Please Note
*Atfer updating information of CTID, the other domain
                        names using the same CTID will be modified.
*Please click the domain name,don't tick the
                        checkbox.(step 3)
*Click the amount of “Current Domain Name”, you will see
                        the list of domain names useing this CTID now (step3)
*If you need to modify VAT Number and Company
                        Name,please refer to"How to Change Owner"
*The domain e-mail can't be modified on system,please
                        refer to“Modify Domain-e-mail address”
*If you have any question, please feel free to contact
                        us.
