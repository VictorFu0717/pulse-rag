FAQ-Update Domain Info (R,A,T,B)
The Steps of Updating Domain Info (R, A, T, B)
The Steps of Updating Domain Info (R, A, T, B)
1. Log in your account
2. Click your account on the top right and choose "Domain
                      Management"
3. Click the domain name.
*You can press “Search” button to search domain name
                      quickly
4.Click "Domain Name Profile" ＞ modify the R, A, T, B
                      information ＞ click "save" after confirming it is correct
Please Note
*please click the domain name directly, don't tick the
                      checkbox. (step 3)
*If you need to modify VAT Number and Company Name, please
                      refer to "How to Change Owner"
*The domain e-mail can't be modified on the system, please
                      refer to “Modify Domain-e-mail address”
If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us.
