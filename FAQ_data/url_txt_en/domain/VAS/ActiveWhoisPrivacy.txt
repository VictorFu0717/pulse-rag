FAQ-How to Enable WHOIS Privacy?
How to Enable WHOIS Privacy?
What is WHOIS Privacy?
· The WHOIS privacy protection service is provided to
                      protect your personal information (Domain name
                      registration information) from being exposed to the public
                      and prevent it from being used by malicious spams or
                      domain name-related harassment.
· If you want to know more details please refer to
WHOIS Privacy. (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/services/domain/WHOISPrivacy#whoisintroduction)
· If you want to know how to buy WHOIS privacy protection
                      service, please refer to "
How to buy (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/faq/domain/VAS/AcquireValueAddService)
".
· If you have already purchased WHOIS Privacy service,
                      please refer to the steps below to activate it.
How to Enable WHOIS Privacy?
1. After purchasing, you will find your domain name with
                      WHOIS privacy protection service in “service to be
                      opened”, and please click “Click to modify” on the right.
2. Click “Enable”.
Attention！
* You can also enable the WHOIS privacy service in “Domain
                      Management”. You can click the red pause button of the
                      domain.
* WHOIS Privacy service is not apply for all kinds of
                      domain.
