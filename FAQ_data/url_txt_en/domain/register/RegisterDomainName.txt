FAQ-How to register a domain name？
How to register a domain name？
Please refer to the following steps to register a domain
                    name.
1. Please log in to your account.
*How to create Net-Chinese account？ (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/faq/membership/account/CreateAccount)
*How to modify account info ? (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/faq/membership/account/CreateAccount)
2. Search the domain name which you want to register on
                      the
Net-Chinese website home page. (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/)
3. Click "Add to Cart" if the domain name is available and
                      click "Check out".
If it shows "Still Want", it means the domain name has
                      been registered. You can refer to "
Buy Back Service (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/services/brand/DomainBuyBack)
" if you still want to buy this domain name.
4. Choose the domain name registration term and click
                      "Next".
5. Click "Choose Value-Added Service" and tick the service
                      which you need and click "Next".
(Click "Next" directly if you don't need any Value-Added
                      Service)
*
CDN Service (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/services/domain/CDN)
*
Redirect (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/services/domain/URLRedirection)
*
WHOIS Privacy (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/services/domain//WHOISPrivacy)
*
Domain Lock (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/services/domain/DomainNameLock)
6. Click User's Agreement and Click "Agree"
*If you choose to use a credit card, you can pay by
                      VISA、Master Card、JCB. Just to remind you that
                      transaction fee and exchange rate fee are extra
                      calculated.
7. Confirm shopping list and click "Check Out"
8. Select a payment method and click "Check Out".
*
How to deposit prepayment to Account (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/faq/order/payment/Prepayment)
*If you choose to use a credit card, you can pay by
                      VISA、Master Card、JCB. Just to remind you that
                      transaction fee and exchange rate fee are extra
                      calculated.
9. You will be directed to the "Services to be opened"
                      page. Please click "Choose Contacts for Domains".
10. Choose or create your domain name contact information
                      and click "Submit domain name contact information".
There are three ways for creating.
Click "Add" and create a new domain name contact
                      information > Click "Create"
If you have created the contact before,you can choose a
                      domain name contact information that you need to use
                      directly. You can click “查看” to check the contact
                      information also.
Fill in the CTID directly and click "ok".
After checking your information, please click "Submit
                      domain name contact information".
Notice
1.CTID is a data code for each domain name information. If
                      you modify it, the other domain names using the same CTID
                      will be modified.
2.If you are still not a member of Net-Chinese, please
                      click "
Sign up (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/nc/index.php/MemRegisterEdit/Index)
" on the top right.
3.Please send an e-mail to us for checking if you have a
                      domain registration identity.
4.Please check the domain name contact information is
                      correct.
