FAQ-How to deposit prepayment？
How to deposit prepayment？
What’s Prepayment？
You can replenish prepayment in your account in advanced
                      for the orders so you will not need to pay by Paypal or
                      credit card every time.
When you register or transfer in domain name, you can only
                      pay the order by Paypal, credit card and prepayment. If
                      you don’t want to use credit card and Paypal, you can
                      deposit prepayment by remittance in advanced.
How to deposit prepayment？
Please log in to your account.
* Retrieve Account ID / Forget Password (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/faq/membership/account/ForgetPassword)
* How to modify account info ? (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/faq/membership/account/ModifyAccountInfo)
Click "Account" > Click your Balance
You can choose the amount or click "other amounts" for
                      entering the amounts which you decide. > Click "Check Out"
Please click "Order Confirmed" after checking the order’s
                      information.
Please select a payment method and click "Check Out".
* Payment methods (https://www.net-chinese.com.tw/en/faq/order/payment/PaymentMethods)
* If you choose to use a credit card, you can pay by
                      【VISA、Master Card、JCB】. Just to remind you that
                      transaction fee and exchange rate fee are extra
                      calculated.
* The order will be kept for 1 month, please finish the
                      payment in the period.
Pay for previous orders
Please log in and click "Order Management".
Find your order and click "Please select payment method".
*You can check the order’s information through click the
                      order number.
Click "Next" after confirming the order’s information.
Select a payment method and click "Check Out".

* If you choose to use a credit card, you can pay by
