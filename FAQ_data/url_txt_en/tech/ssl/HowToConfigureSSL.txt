How To Configure SSL?
How To Configure SSL?
1. Click the ”SSL Certificate” in the menu first, and
                      click “Pending CA”.
2. Click “Configure SSL”
3. Input your CSR：paste the content of CSR and click
                      “Verify CSR” below.(Please generate the CSR document with
                      your server)
Confirm your CSR information and select your server type
                      and authentication option.
4. fill in the corresponding fields with the relevant
                      instructions.
Attention! Please fill in organization information if the
                      certificate require Organization or Extend Validation.
5. Fill in Contact Information of Administrator and
                      Technical, then click "Confirm".
Attention！
Please confirm your domain name and registration
                      information of your certificate are correct.
Please add country code and area code when you enter your
                      phone number of organization information (ex:
                      +886-2-25319696), preventing you from missing the
                      verification phone called by certificate authority.
