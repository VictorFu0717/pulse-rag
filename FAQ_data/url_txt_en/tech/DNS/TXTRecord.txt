FAQ-TXT record
TXT record
How to set TXT record ?
TXT record can provide text information to sources outside
                      your domain.
For example : "v=spf1 include:spf.messaging.microsoft.com
                      ip4:192.168.254.254 -all"
Your email address will be @net-chinese.com after you set
                      a blank A record.
If TXT record is more than 250 characters, please add two
""
to divide it.
For example :
"
v=spf1 include:spf.messaging.
""
microsoft.com ip4:192.168.254.254 -al
"
Notice :
You can set 50 DNS records for free.
If there is any mistake, it would make your record
                      invaild.
