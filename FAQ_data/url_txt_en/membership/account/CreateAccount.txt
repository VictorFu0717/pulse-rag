FAQ-How to create Net-Chinese account？
How to create Net-Chinese account？
Please refer the following steps to create your Net-Chinese
                    account.
1. Click "Sign Up"
2. Fill in the account information > Click "Member Sign
                      Up"
* Account ID should be at least six characters or numbers.
* Password should be a combination of at least eight
                      characters, numbers and a symbol (+-*).
* Verification Codes are four capital letters.
3. If you sign up successfully, the notification will pop
                      up.
Please click notification email to verify your mailbox.
