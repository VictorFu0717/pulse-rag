How to change password？
How to change password？
1. Log in your account.
2. Click "Account" on the top right
3. Click "Reset Password" > Enter "Current Password" and "New Passworld"
* Password should be a combination of at least eight characters, numbers and a symbol (+-*).
4. You will receive an email. Please check email and click the link to verify the password modification.
