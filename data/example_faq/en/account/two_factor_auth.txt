Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Enable 2FA:
1. Log in → Account Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication
2. Click "Enable 2FA"
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.)
4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm setup
5. Save your backup codes in a secure place

Disable 2FA:
1. Log in → Account Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication
2. Click "Disable" and enter your current 2FA code to confirm

Lost access to your authenticator app?
Use one of your saved backup codes on the login page.
If you have no backup codes, contact support@acmetech.example.com with your account ID.

Note: 2FA is mandatory for all Enterprise plan accounts.
