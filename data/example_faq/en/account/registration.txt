Creating an AcmeTech Account

1. Go to acmetech.example.com/signup
2. Enter your email address and choose a secure password (min. 8 characters)
3. Check your email for a verification link and click it within 24 hours
4. Complete your profile: name, company (optional), billing address

Requirements:
- Valid email address (used for login and notifications)
- Password: at least 8 characters, must include letters and numbers

One email address can only be registered once.
If you receive "email already in use", try logging in or use password reset.
