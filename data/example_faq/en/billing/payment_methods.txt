Accepted Payment Methods

Credit / Debit Cards:
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express
- Cards are charged automatically on your billing date

PayPal:
- Link your PayPal account in Billing > Payment Methods

Bank Transfer:
- Available for annual plans over $500
- Contact billing@acmetech.example.com for wire details

Cryptocurrency:
- Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH) accepted
- Requires a 12-month prepayment

All transactions are processed securely. AcmeTech does not store full card numbers.
