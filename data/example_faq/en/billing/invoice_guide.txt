Invoice & Billing FAQ

Q: Where can I download my invoices?
A: Log in → Billing → Invoice History. Invoices are available as PDF.

Q: Can I change the billing name or VAT number on my invoices?
A: Yes. Go to Billing → Company Details and update your information.
   Note: Changes apply to future invoices only. Past invoices cannot be re-issued.

Q: Why was I charged twice this month?
A: This can happen if you upgraded mid-cycle. You are charged a prorated amount
   for the upgraded plan immediately, then the full amount on your next billing date.
   Contact support@acmetech.example.com if you believe it is an error.

Q: How do I get a refund?
A: AcmeTech offers a 30-day money-back guarantee for new subscriptions.
   Submit a refund request at support@acmetech.example.com within 30 days of purchase.
