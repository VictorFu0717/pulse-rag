Managing Your Subscription

Upgrade:
- Log in → Billing → Change Plan → select a higher tier
- Upgrade takes effect immediately; you are charged a prorated amount

Downgrade:
- Log in → Billing → Change Plan → select a lower tier
- Downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing period
- No refund is issued for the unused portion

Cancel:
- Log in → Billing → Cancel Subscription
- Service continues until the end of the paid period
- All data is deleted 30 days after cancellation
- Export your data before cancelling (Settings → Export Data)

Pause (Business and Enterprise only):
- Suspend your account for up to 3 months at 10% of your plan cost
- Contact your account manager or billing@acmetech.example.com
