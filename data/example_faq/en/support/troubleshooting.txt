Common Issues & Solutions

Website shows "Error 500":
- Check your error logs in Control Panel → Logs → Error Log
- Common causes: PHP version mismatch, corrupt .htaccess, exhausted memory limit
- Increase PHP memory limit: add "php_value memory_limit 256M" to .htaccess

Email not sending:
- Verify SMTP settings: host=mail.acmetech.example.com, port=587, STARTTLS
- Check your spam folder
- Ensure your domain's SPF record includes: v=spf1 include:acmetech.example.com ~all

SSL certificate not working:
- Check certificate status in Control Panel → SSL
- If expired, click Renew. Free Let's Encrypt certificates renew automatically
- Clear your browser cache after renewal

Cannot connect via FTP:
- Use SFTP (port 22) instead of plain FTP
- Verify your username and password in Control Panel → FTP Accounts
