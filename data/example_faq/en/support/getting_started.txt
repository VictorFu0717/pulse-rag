Getting Started with AcmeTech

Step 1 — Create your account
  Visit acmetech.example.com/signup, enter your email, and choose a password.

Step 2 — Choose a plan
  Select Starter, Business, or Enterprise. You can upgrade at any time.

Step 3 — Set up your domain
  In the control panel go to Domains → Add Domain. Point your DNS to our nameservers:
    ns1.acmetech.example.com
    ns2.acmetech.example.com
  DNS propagation takes up to 48 hours.

Step 4 — Deploy your website
  Use the one-click installer for WordPress, Joomla, or other CMS platforms,
  or upload files via FTP / SFTP.

Step 5 — Set up email
  Go to Email → Create Mailbox. Enter the mailbox name and a password.
  Configure your mail client using the settings at:
    IMAP: mail.acmetech.example.com:993 (SSL)
    SMTP: mail.acmetech.example.com:587 (STARTTLS)

For video tutorials visit: help.acmetech.example.com/getting-started
